# Service choices and digital authenticity cards

The marketplace now presents a watch buyer's optional services and their starting costs alongside the watch purchase decision. A selected service remains a separate charge from the watch deposit and follows the same watch into the workshop record. The seller view shows the configured commission, authenticity fee and estimated payout before a sale, with a clear distinction between an amount already paid and one still due.

A digital authenticity card is issued only after the linked inspection has been completed and approved. Its public page shows the current card status and a QR that resolves to the same verification page. A later suspension or revocation remains visible through that link. The card does not publish a hidden listing.

## Validation and limits

The scoped source release used hash checks, backups, a rollback rehearsal and a runtime version check. Isolated financial, authorization and certificate lifecycle tests passed. Public marketplace pages rendered in light and dark at mobile and desktop widths without page errors or horizontal overflow; gallery and service selection controls were exercised without submitting a purchase. Real payment callbacks, technician use, message delivery and scans on customer devices still need observation in normal operations. This note does not claim acceptance of those external paths.
