# Smaller watch uploads with high visual quality

New watch photographs can use high-quality visual compression before upload. Large images keep their proportions within a 3200-pixel long edge, and transparency and camera orientation are handled explicitly. The smaller lossless result remains preferred where it is more efficient.

The upload form reports the achieved reduction. Only worthwhile size savings replace a selected file; failed or unsupported processing keeps the original available. Identity documents, payment receipts and existing stored media retain their separate handling.

Quality checks covered a fine scratch and serial crop, a published catalogue photograph, rotated images, transparency, browser fallback and actual multipart upload validation. Seller-form interactions passed mobile and desktop in both themes with compressed replacements and previews. The samples demonstrate this release's tested behavior, not a guaranteed reduction or identical pixels for every photograph.
